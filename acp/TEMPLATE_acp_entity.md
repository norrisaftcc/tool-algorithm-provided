## CLASSIFICATION GUIDE

| Classification | Meaning | Examples |
|----------------|---------|----------|
| **SAFE** | Common, manageable, everyone experiences it | Copy-paste syndrome, Tutorial hell |
| **EUCLID** | Requires active management, can escalate | Impostor pattern, Infinite refactor |
| **KETER** | Potentially serious, may need intervention | Burnout, Severe anxiety, Isolation |

---

## TONE NOTES

- The containment language should be FUN, not frightening
- Always include actionable, practical advice
- Self-deprecating humor works well ("We've all been there")
- KETER-class entities need genuine care mixed with the satire
- The "Instructor Notes" section is for YOU - delete before sharing

---

## NAMING CONVENTIONS

- Three-digit numbers (001, 002, etc.)
- CODENAME should be memorable and descriptive
- Avoid names that could be genuinely hurtful
- Inside jokes are okay if they'll land with the audience
